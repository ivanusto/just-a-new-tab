## 🇺🇸 English (英文版)

### 1. Short Description

> Start your day with a beautiful random wallpaper, elegant clock, quick link shortcuts, and customizable daily quotes.

---

### 2. Detailed Description

**✨ Turn every new tab into a peaceful moment of inspiration.**

《Just a New Tab》is a premium browser extension designed for those who appreciate minimalism, aesthetics, and mindfulness. It replaces the default blank page with a beautiful, glassmorphic dashboard featuring warm greetings, daily quotes, and practical widgets.

Whether you're starting your morning or working late at night, enjoy a clean design with gentle background transitions that bring a peaceful touch to your browsing workflow.

---

### 🌟 Key Features

*   **🖼️ Dynamic Wallpaper Gallery**
    *   **Random Rotation**: Cycles through your enabled wallpapers on every new tab, using a smooth cross-fade transition and a subtle Ken Burns zoom effect to make the page feel alive.
    *   **Premium Default Wallpapers**: Comes with 6 aesthetic, pre-bundled illustrations.
    *   **Custom Uploads**: Drag & drop or click to upload your favorite photos. Select which ones to include in the rotation pool, or delete them instantly with a single click.
*   **✍️ Inspiring Quotes Widget**
    *   **Cloud Fetching**: Enable cloud quotes to pull inspiring thoughts dynamically from the ZenQuotes API.
    *   **Custom Quotes Library**: Add your own favorite quotes and authors. They will rotate randomly along with the default pool.
    *   **Flicker-Free Load**: Optimized rendering fetches quotes instantly from local cache on load, eliminating any 0.x second layout shifts or blinking.
*   **⏰ Elegant Clock & Dynamic Greeting**
    *   **Glassmorphic Clock**: Displays the current time, date, and day of the week with a clean, high-contrast, premium font.
    *   **Smart Greetings**: Dynamically adjusts greeting phrases based on the hour (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night) with custom name personalization support.
*   **🔗 Quick Link Shortcuts (Speed Dial)**
    *   Add, edit, or delete your most visited websites. Cards automatically fetch website favicons, maintaining a clean and beautiful grid.
*   **🎛️ Tailored UI Settings**
    *   Open the side drawer to toggle individual widgets (clock, greeting, quotes, quick links, and zoom effect) on or off according to your preference.

---

### 🔒 Privacy & Performance Commitment

*   **100% Local Storage (Offline-First)**: Your uploaded background images and custom quotes are stored locally inside the browser's **IndexedDB** database. No data is sent or uploaded to external servers. Your privacy is fully secured.
*   **Lightweight & Fast**: Built entirely in vanilla HTML/CSS/JS without heavy frameworks. Opens instantly with zero impact on system memory.
